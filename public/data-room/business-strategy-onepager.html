Tessera — Predict the Close, Price the Value, Expand the Logo

A read-only AI layer above the builder stack that scores every home's on-time-close probability. We charge a fraction of the loss we prevent, run it at near-zero marginal cost, and grow by expanding division-by-division inside builders we already serve. Execution is the moat.

Land-and-expand — the growth engine

  • Land one division. Beachhead = regional / mid-size builders (500–2,000 closings), lower procurement friction, reference case inside the round.
  • Prove lift, then expand. One national builder logo ≈ ~40 divisions — a single logo is a 40-contract runway, not one sale.
  • Net revenue retention >150% comes from division expansion plus the $250 → $650 price walk as more findings ship.
  • Read-only overlay. No rip-and-replace, no new workflow, zero risk to their systems — collapses the IT objection.

Why the ROI compounds — the data moat

  • Every prediction + its outcome is labeled training data. The engine sharpens with every close.
  • Per-tenant models trained inside each builder's account — one builder's data never trains another's (counsel-safe by architecture).
  • The data earned in Layer 1 is what grants the right to build Layer 2 (Home Passport) and Layer 3 (Maintenance AI).
  • Home Passport infra: $0.0046–$0.167/passport/mo. At 1M passports ≈ $10M revenue on ~$55K/yr infra.
