Layer 02 · Built on Layer 1

Home Passport — a Carfax for the home

A persistent digital identity for the home, created from verified builder data and handed to the owner at closing — attached to the property, not the person, and carried across every future sale.
The business case

Every builder hands the homeowner a folder of loose-leaf receipts and walks away. The passport replaces that with a permanent, verifiable record — what's in the home, who installed it, which permits were pulled, when the warranty ends. It's a data asset that no one has built for production homes, and it becomes the system of record the whole lifecycle runs on.

Why the builder wants it (not just us): it's a byproduct of Layer 1, so there's no extra work — while we collect data to predict the close, we're simultaneously assembling the record. And it hands the builder something they've been unable to build internally: a clean handoff that reduces warranty friction and a verified record that de-risks resale, insurance and refinance downstream.

Layer 03 · Built on Layer 2

Marketplace & Maintenance AI — the endless part

Once the home has a verified record, Tessera can serve the homeowner for the life of the property — predictive maintenance, routed services, and a marketplace that monetizes a relationship every big builder is trying to own.
The business case

Post-close monetization is the prize the whole industry is chasing. Large builders have repeatedly launched homeowner marketplaces that stall — because nobody has the unified, verified data to make one work. The passport is that data. Maintenance AI turns it into recurring homeowner engagement, and the marketplace turns engagement into revenue.

Why it's sticky: a first-time buyer doesn't know their AC filter is due, or that they should check the shingles in year two. Tessera predicts it from the home's attested records and cites the source — the kind of “operating system for the house” a homeowner actually keeps using. That daily-life utility is what keeps the homeowner attached to us long after the builder has said “your warranty's up, don't call us.”
🔧

Predictive maintenance

“Your filter is due in 120 days.” “Check your shingles in year two.” Each reminder cites the record it came from — delivered by email/push, no app to check.

Model: per-homeowner subscription · recurring engagement
🔗

Direct-to-trade service routing

A service need routes homeowner → trade directly, instead of homeowner → builder as a warranty ticket. Less friction for the builder, faster fix for the owner.

Model: lead / take-rate to preferred trades
🤝

Services marketplace & JVs

The clean data fast-tracks the joint ventures builders already chase — home insurance, energy, and services partnerships — with the record as the connective tissue.

Model: marketplace rev-share · insurers, energy, services
