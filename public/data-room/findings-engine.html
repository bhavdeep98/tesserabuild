Tessera:
A Findings Engine

We ship one product surface. The pipeline underneath supports two dozen.

Author: Engineering · Status: Proposal

Summary

We ship one product surface: a Closing Confidence Score. The pipeline underneath it supports roughly two dozen distinct findings. The method that unlocks them is neuro-symbolic: a symbolic scaffold of domain axioms, postulates, and a dependency graph, with state-of-the-art models reasoning inside it. The scaffold sets what is possible, the models find what is likely, and together they surface interactions across 51 signal types and six domains that no superintendent would catch by inspection.

Context

Running today: seven read-only connectors above the builder's stack, 51 signal types, a 45-node dependency graph per home, gradient-boosted classification with SHAP attribution, anomaly detection, and a 500-home reference corpus across 3 divisions and 15 communities.

We have never enumerated what that data can prove. The score answers one question and flattens all the evidence into a single integer, while the pipeline quietly retains a full event log per home, every revision to every declared close date, cohort keys down to community and plan, and monetary fields nothing reads.

The method

Findings are derived, not reported. The structure is Euclidean, and the models work inside it.

Symbolic scaffold

Axioms What a connected system observes: event times, confirmed absences, declared dates, revisions, amounts
→
Postulates Construction has fixed physical order. Same community and plan shares a duration distribution. Lending has a minimum duration.
→
Lemmas Built once, reused everywhere: phase intervals, cohort distributions, precedence closure, critical path
↓Graph topology handed down as features
↑Physical coherence filters what comes back

Learned models

Structural features Critical path position and precedence depth, not recoverable from counts and ratios
→
Inference Probability and effect size inside the bounds the scaffold sets
→
Hypothesis search High-order interactions across signals, cohorts, and topology
Theorems: the findings

Each one traceable to the axioms, postulates, and inferences it consumed. Every finding declares its basis.

Constraint · bounded by postulates and graph Cohort · statistical against peers Hybrid · inference inside symbolic bounds Model · learned

The two layers compound rather than compete. The graph masks predictions that are physically impossible, so drywall never precedes framing. Cross-domain couplings the template already encodes, such as drywall completion gating the appraisal, are handed over as prior structure instead of left for a model to rediscover. And a candidate pattern only becomes a finding once it holds up against the graph and the postulates, which is what keeps false positives low enough for an operator to keep trusting the alerts.

Coverage is therefore enumerable. Salesforce alone audits schedule credibility. Add any construction system and a close date becomes falsifiable. Attributing a delay to a domain requires two domains connected. That turns integration talks into a specific list per connector.

Highest leverage item is schedule calibration: measured actuals written back into the template. Expected durations and lag days are hand-authored constants today, and every date we emit inherits their error.

Where the analytics goes next

These compose the same lemmas into heavier machinery. Each is a distinct sale.
Finding Example Mechanism
Graph-aware scoring Needs precedence closure Two deals both sit at 60% complete and score differently, because one's remaining work is on the critical path and the other's is not. Critical path position, precedence depth, and centrality added to the feature vector
Schedule risk simulation Needs calibrated distributions If the drywall crew slips a week in Coral Springs, 14 closings move and $1.9M shifts out of the quarter. Monte Carlo across the dependency graph using per-phase duration distributions
Intervention ranking Needs calibrated graph Ordering the appraisal today recovers 11 days. Expediting the punch list recovers 2. Do the first one. Recompute critical path under perturbed milestone timings, diff the outcomes
Close date as a range, not a class label Needs transition history Lot 118 closes between Sept 28 and Oct 14 at 80% confidence, instead of being labelled AT_RISK. Survival model over right-censored phase transitions
Causal path attribution Needs outcome volume Late selections cost you 8 days, and the path runs through drywall gating the appraisal, not through construction directly. Effect sizes estimated along specific graph edges, so a driver names its mechanism
Hypothesis discovery Needs structural features When HVAC rough-in trails electrical by more than 6 days and the appraisal is still open, close dates slip 18 days. Nobody was tracking that pair. Frontier math and analytics models search high-order interactions; the scaffold filters for physical coherence
Learned postulates Needs history at volume The data says permit revision consistently precedes foundation by 9 days in Broward. Confirm it and we add the edge to the graph. Dependencies inferred from consistent co-occurrence and lag, proposed as graph edges for human ratification
Partner scorecards Needs partner ID extraction Lender A clears underwriting in 24 days. Lender B takes 39, on the same borrower profiles. Cycle time and pull-through per partner identifier lifted from payloads
Per-home margin waterfall Needs amount extraction The Magnolia pro forma says 21% margin. Actuals land at 16%, and carry plus change orders account for the whole gap. Change order amounts, cost variance, carry, and incentives joined per home
Absorption versus build capacity Needs throughput series You are signing 14 homes a month in Sawgrass and completing 9. Dates promised past March are not achievable. Signing rate extrapolated against completion throughput to forecast backlog and date feasibility
Market benchmarking across builders Needs multi-tenant volume Your title step runs 40% slower than the market median for comparable Florida communities. Anonymized cohort distributions across tenants

Two of these change the product's category. Schedule risk simulation and intervention ranking move us from reporting on a deal to testing a decision before it is made.

One compounds indefinitely. Learned postulates mean the symbolic layer grows from the data it ingests: models infer candidate rules, humans ratify them, the graph gets richer, and every model trained on that graph gets sharper. Volume does not just improve accuracy, it expands what the system can prove.
