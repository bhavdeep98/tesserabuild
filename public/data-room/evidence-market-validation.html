3 · Pitch numbers — verified & conservative

Source verification for the figures used across the deck and whitepaper.
Carry cost per day — why we use $500

The published figure is ~$670/day (Association of Professional Builders, on a $750K build). Scaled to a $370K production home that's ~$330/day of hard cost; opportunity cost and incentive-erosion risk push the fully-loaded figure to $500–$700/day. We standardize on $500/day — deliberately at the low end, and well below the retired $2,000/day deck number — so the business case never depends on the aggressive end of the range.

4 · Why now

The pain is acute, the industry is asking for it, and the systems are finally reachable.
Record cancellations — the pain is acute and worsening (20% at DHI; ~14% nationally)
Margin compression — builders spending 2–3× normal on incentives just to move inventory
AI readiness — the industry is openly calling for operational intelligence inside the ERP layer
System maturity — BuildPro, Encompass, Salesforce, Procore all have APIs we can read
Cycle-time awareness — leaders like Meritage prove coordination is the competitive differentiator

Nobody has built the cross-system dependency graph connecting construction → mortgage → title → CRM into a single predictive intelligence layer. That is the gap Tessera fills.
