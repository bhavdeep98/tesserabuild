Why it matters to the model

The "4 clients/yr" assumption stays the conservative floor. But because a client can be a division inside a company we already serve, expansion revenue compounds without new logo acquisition — the growth engine runs on net revenue retention, not just new-logo count.

Translating the model down to the company

The base case uses a clean $400K operating-cost placeholder. Here's the honest breakdown behind it. The per-client cost is operational cost, distributed — AWS infrastructure is only the floor; the larger piece is the human effort to run each active account (monitoring, support, signal upkeep). The business case holds even with honest numbers because that operational cost is small next to the license revenue each client generates.
