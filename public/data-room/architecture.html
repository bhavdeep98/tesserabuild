Architecture · one product

One Home Passport. One system. Several modules underneath.

What the VC should see first: a single property identity that builders, owners, and providers share. Under the hood we split work into APIs — not into three products.

Product = Home Passport. Close intelligence, identity/permissions, and ledger fingerprints are internal modules of that product. Tessera Front End is how you demo the same home across roles.
