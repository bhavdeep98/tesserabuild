Three beats · ~3 minutes

1

Builder — see the close at risk

Sign in as the builder. Open Outlook / construction for a lot. Predictability (Kestrel) surfaces cross-system risk — house ready, loan or title not — on the same home identity.

2

Handoff — the Passport stays with the home

Move through handoff. The record is not a forked binder for a new login; it is the property’s continuous Passport that the owner will claim.

3

Homeowner + provider — act on that identity

Sign in as Maya (owner) for passport / care / move-in. Sign in as Jordan (provider) for a job that only receives what the grant allows. Same home. Different authority.
