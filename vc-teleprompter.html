Likely questions
“If the key goes to the homeowner, does the builder lose the data?” (Maen asked this exact thing)
No. The chain entry is just a public fingerprint. What it unlocks — and where the real record lives — stays controlled. The builder keeps their copy; a copy hands off to the homeowner. Both can use their data. Nothing sensitive is ever on the chain.
“Why blockchain at all?”
Because it gives a record that persists and stays trustworthy even as ownership changes — the provenance layer. It also happens to unlock crypto payments in markets like Dubai, but that’s a bonus, not the reason.
